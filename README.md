## Welcome to my te reo māori Kīwaha Desktop App!

### Why this app?

I'm thirty and I want to solidify some learning of te reo māori, specifically kīwaha AKA common phrases. I feel they are a nice entry way to using more reo in your day to day. Hopefully throughout the course of developing this app I will discover more kīwaha I can use and love. I whakapapa to the east coast of Aotearoa (Ngati Kahungunu O Te Wairoa) so it would be great to incoorporate lots of kīwaha that are native to that region.

### Who is it for and what do you hope to learn?

Literally anyone but mostly me haha. 
I'm not so good with my JS logic so I'm hoping to solidify some learning. script.js will hopefully be something I am proud of in the end. Additionally I would like the app to feature some really slick UI, so it can look pretty and be something I actually use.
